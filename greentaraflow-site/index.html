ABOUT

I teach what I'm still learning.

I'm Consuelo, and I teach from Malmö. Green Tara Flow is where I share the practice as I go, not once I've finished.

Yoga found me at a time when I lived mostly in my head. I went to my first classes to stretch a body that sat at a screen all day and to rest a mind that was burned out. Movement, breath and meditation gave me a way back, and I wanted to pass that on. I completed my 200-hour teacher training at Breathe and Flow Yoga Academy, and I'm studying yin now. What I teach from:

  • Body, mind and breath are one practice.
  • Presence over perfection.
  • Slow is where you start to notice.

Why the name Green Tara

Green Tara is the one who acts. In Tibetan Buddhism she is compassion in motion: she sits in stillness with one foot already stepping down, ready to help. That is what I want from a practice, a stillness that makes you more ready for your life, not less.

The flower is the utpala, the blue night lotus she holds in her left hand.
